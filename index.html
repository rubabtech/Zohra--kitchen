About Us

Meet Esha Ahsan

Zohra Kitchen is founded by Esha Ahsan, a passionate home baker who puts her heart into every cake she creates. With over 1.5 years of experience, she has built a special place in the hearts of Lahore's cake lovers.

Every cake is made with 100% fresh cream — no artificial ingredients, no compromises. Every order is fully customized — your design, your flavor, your vision!

Whether it's a birthday, wedding, Eid, or any special occasion — Zohra Kitchen makes every moment sweeter and more memorable!
